RadioCult has launched 24/7 Music Recognition, an add-on that uses real-time audio fingerprinting to automatically detect and log every track played on a station. The system eliminates manual royalty reporting while maintaining accurate metadata, powered by ACRCloud's technology with access to 100 million audio tracks.
